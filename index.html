Hi, I'm Athulya 👋

A data and strategy enthusiast who finds patterns not just in numbers, but in stories. With 5+ years of experience in analytics, process optimization, and experimentation, I’ve built a career around using data to uncover insights that make business decisions smarter and customer experiences better.

My path has been shaped by curiosity and diverse experiences — having schooled across Calicut, Cochin, and the UAE, I developed a flexible and adaptive mindset early on. After completing my undergraduate studies at NIT Calicut, I explored the analyst world in Bangalore, realized the power of consulting to work on diverse projects, and discovered my love for data-driven decision-making. This led me to pursue an MSBA at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, complemented by a part-time data engineer intern at Generac, and eventually as a BIE at Amazon India.

Outside work, I nurture creativity and mindfulness through Indian classical Bharatanatyam, poetry, and reading. I enjoy solving Sudoku puzzles, exploring new stories on Netflix, and finding inspiration in the small yet meaningful moments of life.

My journey has equipped me with technical skills and expertise, enabling me to tackle diverse challenges. I have led workshops, conducted research, and mentored colleagues, all of which have enriched my ability to communicate complex ideas with clarity. I thrive on challenging projects and continuous learning, always staying ahead in this ever-evolving field. I'm excited to collaborate with organizations to achieve goals through data-driven decision-making!
