Hi, I'm Athulya 👋

A data and strategy enthusiast who finds patterns not just in numbers, but in stories. With 5+ years of experience in analytics, process optimization, and experimentation, I’ve built a career around using data to uncover insights that make business decisions smarter and customer experiences better.

My path has been shaped by curiosity and diverse experiences — growing up across Calicut, Cochin, and the UAE taught me adaptability and openness early on. After graduating from NIT Calicut, I began my career in Bengaluru beginning with exploring the analyst world, later the power of consulting exposed me to work on diverse projects and sparked my passion for data-driven decision-making. This led me to pursue an MSBA at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, complemented by a part-time data engineer intern at Generac, and eventually as a BIE at Amazon India.

Outside work, I nurture creativity and mindfulness through Indian classical Bharatanatyam, yoga, poetry, and reading. I enjoy solving Sudoku puzzles, exploring new stories on Netflix, and finding inspiration in the small yet meaningful moments of life.

My journey has strengthened my technical expertise and ability to communicate complex ideas with clarity. I thrive on challenging projects, continuous learning, and collaboration — and I’m excited to bring analytical rigor and creative thinking together to solve meaningful problems.
