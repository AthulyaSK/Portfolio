Hi, I'm Athulya 👋

I am an analytically driven and results-oriented professional with 5+ years of experience in data, strategy, and process optimization. Beyond numbers and analytics, I’m passionate about creating meaningful insights that drive business growth and improve customer experiences.

My journey has been shaped by diverse experiences—having studied across Calicut, Cochin, and Dubai, I developed a flexible and adaptive mindset early on. After completing my undergraduate studies at NIT Calicut, I explored the IT world in Bangalore, realized the power of consulting to work on diverse projects, and discovered my love for data-driven decision-making. This led me to pursue an MSBA at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, complemented by a part-time data engineer intern at Generac, and eventually as a BIE at Amazon India.

Outside work, I nurture creativity and mindfulness through Indian classical Bharatanatyam, poetry, and reading. I enjoy solving Sudoku puzzles, exploring new stories on Netflix, and finding inspiration in the small yet meaningful moments of life.

I’m now seeking my next opportunity to combine analytical rigor with strategic thinking and continue solving complex business challenges, while bringing curiosity, creativity, and passion to everything I do.
